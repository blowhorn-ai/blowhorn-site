# Built for you and the AI agents that work for you

Blowhorn is built so AI agents can run your marketing as safely as you can. Every action is a command, every write is a rehearsal first, and every rule you set applies to agents too.

## Two ways in for agents

- [blowhorn-axi](https://blowhorn.ai/ai/blowhorn-axi/) (Available now): A command your agent runs to see your profiles and act on your behalf, with answers written for agents to read.
- [The Blowhorn MCP server](https://blowhorn.ai/ai/mcp/) (Available now): A standard plug that lets AI assistants find every Blowhorn action and use it, with your approval rules built in.

## Three promises to every human in charge

- **Rehearsal first.** Any action an agent asks for is a dry run until it is told to go. It sees exactly what would happen before anything is posted.
- **Your limits, every time.** The risk limits you set for each profile apply to every agent action. An agent can't post more, or faster, than you allow.
- **Runs on your computer.** Agents drive Blowhorn on your own computer, from your own browser. Your passwords never leave your computer.
