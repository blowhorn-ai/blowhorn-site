# Blowhorn vs Postiz

> Postiz is the strongest agent-ready scheduler out there, with 30+ networks, a hosted MCP and a self-host option. Blowhorn's difference is who presses Post: your own browser, on your own computers, plus Hacker News, GitHub and LinkedIn outreach.

## Choose Postiz if

- You want 30+ networks, including Discord, Instagram and TikTok
- You want to self-host open source software
- Posts must go out with every laptop closed

[Postiz](https://postiz.com/)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- You want posts to come from your own signed-in browser, not a vendor's app
- Hacker News, GitHub and careful LinkedIn outreach matter to you
- You run a fleet of computers and want to pause one action, one computer or all of them

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

Postiz and Blowhorn agree more than they disagree: neither sells engagement. Our bet is on people. Blowhorn's sound wave includes the profiles your teammates and supporters add themselves, so the replies under your post come from people who actually chose to stand behind it.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#questions}

**Is Postiz better than Blowhorn?** For breadth, self-hosting and one-click agent connectors, Postiz is ahead today, and it's a great choice. For acting from your own browser, Hacker News, GitHub, LinkedIn outreach and a fleet of your own computers, we think Blowhorn is the better fit.

**Does Blowhorn have an MCP server?** Yes. A local MCP server ships with the desktop app (blowhorn-mcp, one tool per command, with the risky ones hidden until you opt in). Every write is a dry run until the assistant confirms it, and an agent can never approve its own content. Postiz is ahead on hosted MCP today; Blowhorn's hosted server is coming soon.
