# Blowhorn vs Sociabble

> Blowhorn isn't a bot. It's you. Sociabble - now the Poppulo Experience Platform - combines advocacy with intranet, internal comms and recognition for large companies. Blowhorn is the small, fast way for technical teams to amplify launches.

## Choose Sociabble if

- You need advocacy plus intranet, internal comms and recognition in one platform
- You run a large, multi-site or frontline workforce
- Approved content and governance come first

[Sociabble](https://www.sociabble.com/)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- You are a founder, a DevRel team, a practice, an agency or a growing company
- Your people talk on LinkedIn and X, and on Hacker News, Reddit, GitHub, Slack and Bluesky
- You want amplification working today, without a sales call or a seat-count quote

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

Advocacy suites are right that people outperform brand accounts. Blowhorn takes that idea to smaller technical teams: real people, acting as themselves, from their own computers, on the networks developers use.
