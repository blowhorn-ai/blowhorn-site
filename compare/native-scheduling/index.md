# Blowhorn vs native scheduling

> LinkedIn and X let you schedule posts for free, one network and one profile at a time. Blowhorn puts every network and every profile you run in one queue, and lets your profiles amplify each post.

## Choose native scheduling if

- You post on one network from one profile
- Free, official and zero setup matter most
- You don't need anyone to amplify your posts

[native scheduling](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1524001)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- You post the same news across LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub, Bluesky and Slack
- You run several profiles and want them to amplify each other
- You want one place to see LinkedIn, X and Bluesky results

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

A post tends to do best when real people react early. Blowhorn helps the profiles you run show up on time, as themselves, with no bought likes.
