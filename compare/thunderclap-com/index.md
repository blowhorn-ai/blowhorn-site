## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

Bought followers and likes move a number. They rarely move a person. X's rules prohibit buying or selling engagement, and LinkedIn says it limits inauthentic engagement. We think the real prize is strong signal: someone who knows your work replying, asking a hard question, sharing it with their own take. You can't buy that, so we don't sell it.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#questions}

**Is Thunderclap.com the original Thunderclap?** No. The original crowdspeaking service ran at thunderclap.it and closed in 2018. Thunderclap.com's own page says the domain was bought by a different company after the shutdown.

**Does Blowhorn sell followers or likes?** No, and it never will. Blowhorn amplifies through profiles that you, your teammates and your supporters add to your account.

**Is Thunderclap.com better than Blowhorn?** If a fast number is what you need, it does what it says. If you want real people and real conversation, we think Blowhorn is the better fit.
