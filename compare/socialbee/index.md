# Blowhorn vs SocialBee

> SocialBee is a tidy category-based scheduler for small businesses and agencies. Blowhorn is for technical teams who need real people amplifying on developer networks.

## Choose SocialBee if

- You run a small business or agency with evergreen content categories
- You want queues that refill themselves and an AI copilot
- Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest and Google Business matter

[SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- Launches, releases and posts that need your people behind them
- Hacker News, Reddit, GitHub and Slack in the same queue
- An agent-friendly CLI and one queue across all your computers

## Side by side

| Price | Per plan and profile count | Free plan available, see pricing |

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

A steady feed is good hygiene. The posts that move a technical audience are the ones people argue about in the replies, and those need real people showing up.
