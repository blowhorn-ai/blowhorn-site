# Blowhorn vs Claude in Chrome

> Claude in Chrome is a general browser agent that can do almost anything you can, in your own logins, and it is genuinely good at that. Blowhorn isn't a bot. It's you: a dedicated social tool with a queue, a schedule, amplification and scripted, rehearsable actions, and Claude Code can drive it from the command line.

## Choose Claude in Chrome if

- You want one assistant for any browser task, not just social
- Occasional, one-off posts are all you need
- You already pay for Claude and want to stay in one tool

[Claude in Chrome](https://claude.com/claude-in-chrome)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- You want a queue, a schedule, many profiles and many computers
- Actions should be scripted and rehearsed with --dry-run, not decided click by click
- You want your agent to drive Blowhorn instead of driving the browser itself

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

Platforms are cracking down on bots, and the usual fixes look like bots too. For a voice that matters, you want predictable, paced actions from your own signed-in browser and real people in the replies. Blowhorn gives Claude a clean set of commands so it can help you do exactly that.
