# Blowhorn vs DSMN8

> Blowhorn isn't a bot. It's you. DSMN8 is an employee advocacy and executive influence platform for mid-market and enterprise, with governed programs, approvals and analytics. Blowhorn is a lighter way for small technical teams to amplify through real people on developer networks, free during early access.

## Choose DSMN8 if

- You want a governed advocacy program: approvals, SSO, audit trails and gamification
- Executive influence and newsletters are part of the program
- Unlimited admin seats and full onboarding matter
- Annual contracts and a demo-led purchase suit your procurement

[DSMN8](https://dsmn8.com/)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- You are a small technical team, not an enterprise rollout
- Your audience is on Hacker News, Reddit, GitHub, LinkedIn, X and Bluesky, and you use Slack messages via API
- You want to start free during early access, with no sales call in the way, knowing sessions can break, automation can still be blocked, and a computer must be awake because this is not hosted SaaS

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

Advocacy works because real people are trusted. DSMN8 governs that trust for large programs. Blowhorn keeps the real-people part and drops the program overhead for teams that just need their people behind a post, free during early access, with no tiers.
