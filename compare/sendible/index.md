# Blowhorn vs Sendible

> Sendible is a polished cloud scheduler for agencies and marketing teams, with unlimited users, white-label client dashboards and reporting. Blowhorn is for real-person amplification on the networks where developers talk.

## Choose Sendible if

- You run an agency and need white-label dashboards and client reporting
- You want unlimited team members without per-seat fees
- You need scheduled publishing across the mainstream social networks

[Sendible](https://www.sendible.com/)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- One post should be lifted by many real profiles, not scheduled
- Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub and Slack are where your audience is
- You want posting to come from your own Chrome, signed in as you

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

Sendible proves the work got done: scheduled, approved, reported, all under the agency's own brand. It doesn't start a discussion. Blowhorn spends its effort on the first hour of a post that matters, when real people replying is what gets it seen.
