# Blowhorn vs Buffer

> Blowhorn isn't a bot. It's you. Buffer is the friendliest way to keep a steady, approved calendar on many networks, with every computer switched off. Blowhorn is for the moment you need many real voices, replying as themselves, on the networks developers actually read.

## Choose Buffer if

- You want a calm calendar across many consumer networks, with approvals for your team
- Posts must go out with every computer switched off
- You want a free plan to start, and per-channel pricing that grows one channel at a time

[Buffer](https://buffer.com/)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- Your audience lives on LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub, Bluesky and Slack
- One post should be amplified by your founders, your team and your supporters, as themselves, not just published once
- You want posting to happen from your own browser, on your own computers, at a human pace, under rules you set
- You run one person, a local practice, a whole enterprise, or several client brands from one shared queue

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

Buffer is honest software and it sells no engagement either, so this one is about reach, not ethics. A calendar publishes your post once, from one account. Feeds move on replies and real conversation in the first hour, from people who know you. Platforms are cracking down on anything that looks like a bot, and doing it all by hand does not scale. That gap is the job Blowhorn was built for: your own voice, at a human pace, amplified by real people.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#questions}

**Is Buffer better than Blowhorn?** For a broad, always-on calendar across consumer networks, yes. For amplifying one message from many real profiles on LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub, Bluesky and Slack, we think Blowhorn is the better fit.

**Can I use both?** Yes. Plenty of teams keep a calendar tool for the brand feed and use Blowhorn to rally the people behind a launch.

**Does Blowhorn post while every computer is switched off?** No. As long as one computer set up for the profile is awake, the schedule runs. With every computer switched off, nothing goes out. That is the honest trade for posting as you, from your own browser.
