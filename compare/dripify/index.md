# Blowhorn vs Dripify

> Blowhorn isn't a bot. It's you. Dripify is a capable LinkedIn and email drip campaign tool for sales teams, run from its cloud with a Smart Inbox for replies. If simple, always-on drip sequences are the job, pick Dripify, honestly. Blowhorn is for careful outreach and real-person amplification, from your own browser, on seven networks.

## Choose Dripify if

- Your sales team runs drip sequences on LinkedIn and email
- You want team management and campaign analytics (team management on Advanced)
- Cloud execution is what you want, with campaigns running while every computer is closed

[Dripify](https://dripify.io/)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- You want careful LinkedIn hygiene, not volume prospecting
- No vendor server should log in as you; Blowhorn acts from your own signed-in browser
- You also speak on X, Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub, Bluesky and Slack, and want your people amplifying
- You want safety controls, a dry run before anything posts, and one pause switch for the whole fleet

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

A drip sequence is a script for many strangers. Blowhorn spends its effort on posts that people who know you want to reply to. Platforms are cracking down on bots, and the usual fixes look like bots. Doing it by hand doesn't scale, and likes don't move feeds - replies do.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#questions}

**Is Dripify better than Blowhorn?** For sales drip sequences, yes. For publishing and amplifying across many networks from your own browser, we think Blowhorn is the better fit.

**Does Dripify keep running with my computer closed?** Yes, from its cloud, with no browser extension required and no computer left open. Blowhorn needs at least one of your computers awake, because it acts from your own browser.

**Does Dripify amplify my posts?** Not the way Blowhorn does. Dripify has no pods, no post boosting and no content scheduling in its documented features. Liking a post can be a step inside a sequence, but that is not amplification of your posts by your people.
