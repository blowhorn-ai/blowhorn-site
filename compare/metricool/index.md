# Blowhorn vs Metricool

> Blowhorn isn't a bot. It's you. Metricool wins on analytics, competitor tracking and ads reporting for brands and agencies. Blowhorn is amplification-first, through real people on developer networks.

## Choose Metricool if

- Analytics, competitor tracking and ads reporting drive your work
- You manage many brands as an agency
- Instagram, TikTok and YouTube are core channels, with Twitch analytics and competitor tracking

[Metricool](https://metricool.com/)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- You need many real profiles to lift one post
- Hacker News, Reddit, GitHub and Slack message surfaces matter
- Your own browser for LinkedIn, X, Reddit and Hacker News; the platform API for GitHub, Bluesky and Slack

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

Metricool tells you which post landed. Blowhorn is built for the post before that: one message carried by many real profiles, not one brand profile measured after the fact.
