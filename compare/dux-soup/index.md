# Blowhorn vs Dux-Soup

> Blowhorn isn't a bot. It's you. Dux-Soup, now sold as STACKED, wins at LinkedIn drip campaigns with CRM sync, so if volume prospecting on LinkedIn is the job, pick it. Blowhorn is for careful outreach and posts that earn real replies, across many networks, from your own computers.

## Choose Dux-Soup if

- You want LinkedIn drip campaigns: visits, connects and follow-ups in one sequence
- CRM sync matters: leads and replies flow into HubSpot, Salesforce or Pipedrive
- A desktop app on your computer suits you, or a cloud tier that keeps running without it
- LinkedIn is your only channel, including Sales Navigator or Recruiter

[Dux-Soup](https://www.dux-soup.com/)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- You want careful LinkedIn outreach, not volume prospecting
- No vendor server logs in as you. Blowhorn acts from your own signed-in browser, on your own computers
- You also speak on X, Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub, Bluesky and Slack, and you want your people amplifying your posts as themselves
- You are a solo founder, a local practice, a big team or an agency, and you want one queue for every profile

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

Visits and connects start a relationship, they are not the whole of one. Blowhorn spends its effort where posts earn replies: real conversation in the first hour, from people who know you.
