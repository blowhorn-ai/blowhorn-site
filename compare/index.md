## Why it matters

- **It's you, not a bot.** Blowhorn drives the browser you already sign in to. No server logs in with your password, session cookie or 2FA secret. [Details](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/explanation/your-own-chrome/)
- **One queue, every computer.** Add computers for coverage. Each action is claimed by one computer, and whichever computer is awake keeps the schedule. [Details](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/explanation/scheduling/)
- **A fleet behind every post.** Amplify reposts and comments from every profile you run while the post is still fresh. Teammates joining your fleet is coming soon. [Details](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/explanation/distribution/)
- **Your hand on the switch.** Pause one action, one computer or every computer at once, with the reason recorded. Resume and due times carry forward. [Details](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/explanation/youre-in-control/)
- **Careful by default.** Human-paced typing, built-in caps, one click per run, one automation per platform per profile at a time. [Details](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/explanation/reliability-and-anti-bot-design/)
- **Where technical teams talk.** Hacker News, Reddit, GitHub and Slack sit in the same queue as LinkedIn, X and Bluesky. [Details](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/reference/platforms/)

## Credit where due

- Cloud schedulers post with every laptop closed and cover more networks today, with calendars, approvals and inboxes Blowhorn doesn't try to match.
- LinkedIn growth tools run high-volume outbound sequences. Blowhorn does careful LinkedIn outreach, not a sequencer.
- Employee advocacy suites bring approvals, content libraries and compliance archiving for large programs.
- Local multi-profile browsers run on more operating systems. Blowhorn runs on your computer ([System requirements](/docs/reference/requirements/)).
