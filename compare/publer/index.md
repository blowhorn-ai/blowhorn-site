# Blowhorn vs Publer

> Blowhorn isn't a bot. It's you. Publer wins on budget scheduling, recycling and bulk posting across 13 networks. Blowhorn is for real-person amplification on the networks where developers talk.

## Choose Publer if

- You want budget-friendly scheduling across many networks, including Telegram and WordPress
- Bulk scheduling, recycling and RSS automation save you hours
- You need a cloud publishing pipeline across many networks

[Publer](https://publer.com/)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- One post should be lifted by many real profiles, not recycled
- Hacker News, Reddit, GitHub and Slack message surfaces are where your audience is
- Your own browser for LinkedIn, X, Reddit and Hacker News; the platform API for GitHub, Bluesky and Slack

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

Recycling evergreen posts keeps a feed alive. It doesn't start a discussion. Blowhorn spends its effort on posts that matter, where a real reply matters more than a recycled post.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#questions}

**Is Publer better than Blowhorn?** For affordable, high-volume scheduling across many networks, yes. For amplifying a few posts that really matter through real people, we think Blowhorn is the better fit.

**Does Publer have a unified inbox?** No unified inbox; replies happen in native apps. Blowhorn does not claim a care desk either: it amplifies one message through many real profiles.
