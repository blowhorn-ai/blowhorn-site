# Blowhorn vs Expandi

> Blowhorn isn't a bot. It's you. Expandi is a serious outbound machine: LinkedIn and email sequences, branching Smart Sequences, InMails and personalization at volume, run from its cloud. If that volume is the job, pick Expandi, honestly. Blowhorn is for careful outreach and real-person amplification, from your own browser, on seven networks.

## Choose Expandi if

- You run outbound sales and need multi-step LinkedIn and email sequences with branching and A/B tests
- You want agency white label and multi-account management (Agency tier)
- Volume matters more than anything else, and you want campaigns running with every laptop closed

[Expandi](https://expandi.io/)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- You want careful LinkedIn hygiene, not volume prospecting
- No vendor server should log in as you; Blowhorn acts from your own signed-in browser
- You also speak on X, Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub, Bluesky and Slack, and want your people amplifying
- You want safety controls, a dry run before anything posts, and one pause switch for the whole fleet

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

Outbound volume fills a pipeline. Discussion builds a reputation. Platforms are cracking down on bots, and the usual fixes look like bots. Doing it by hand doesn't scale, and likes don't move feeds - replies do. Blowhorn caps itself (20 LinkedIn invitations per run, for example) because a few real conversations beat a thousand ignored invites.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#questions}

**Is Expandi better than Blowhorn?** For outbound sequences at volume, yes, and Blowhorn won't pretend otherwise. For careful outreach from your own browser plus publishing and amplification across many networks, we think Blowhorn is the better fit.

**Is Expandi safe, or approved by LinkedIn?** Expandi itself says LinkedIn's rules strictly prohibit unauthorized automation and no tool or setting guarantees complete protection. Blowhorn makes no stronger claim: it is built to keep your profile within each platform's limits, with human pacing, caps and a dry run first.

**Does Expandi amplify my posts?** Not the way Blowhorn does. Expandi has no pod network and sells no engagement, but its Post Engagement campaigns target people who liked or commented on a post. Blowhorn's sound wave sends one message out through every profile in your account, as themselves.
