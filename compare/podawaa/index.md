# Blowhorn vs Podawaa

> Podawaa is a LinkedIn engagement exchange: members like and comment on each other's posts, with AI comments and paid private options. Blowhorn sells no engagement and focuses on real people who know you.

## Choose Podawaa if

- You want automatic early engagement on every LinkedIn post
- A free community plan to try it suits you
- You've weighed LinkedIn's stated policy on pods

[Podawaa](https://www.podawaa.com/)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- You want people who actually know you to show up, not a reciprocal exchange with strangers
- You want to write your own words, not AI-generated comments
- You also speak on X, Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub, Bluesky and Slack

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

An exchange guarantees activity, not interest. The comment that matters is the one from someone who read your post and has something to say. Blowhorn is built to get your post in front of those people, through the profiles of those who already know you.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#questions}

**Is Podawaa better than Blowhorn?** If you want guaranteed early likes and comments on LinkedIn and accept the trade-offs, it does that. If you want real people and real conversation across many networks, we think Blowhorn is the better fit.

**What does LinkedIn say about pods?** On 13 Mar 2026 LinkedIn wrote that engagement pods are not allowed and that it limits the reach of automated comments. Every platform has rules about automation and coordinated activity; read them, whichever tool you use.
