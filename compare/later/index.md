# Blowhorn vs Later

> Blowhorn isn't a bot. It's you. Later wins the visual game: a polished planner for Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest, with a link-in-bio page and creator analytics. If your audience scrolls pictures, pick Later and don't look back. Blowhorn is for the other pain: technical launches that die without replies from real people on LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Hacker News and GitHub, and doing that by hand doesn't scale.

## Choose Later if

- Your brand lives on Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, Threads, YouTube or Snapchat
- A visual calendar, a link-in-bio page and creator analytics matter to you
- You want team approvals and posting with every computer closed

[Later](https://later.com/)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- Your audience is engineers and builders on LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub and Bluesky
- You want your team and supporters amplifying each launch as themselves
- You want your AI agent driving a real CLI, with you holding the pause switch

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

Later helps a brand look good. Blowhorn helps a message get answered. On developer networks, a single reply from someone who builds with you outweighs a pile of passive likes.
