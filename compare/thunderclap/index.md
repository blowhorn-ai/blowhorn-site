# Blowhorn vs Thunderclap

> Thunderclap (thunderclap.it, 2012 to 2018) proved that a crowd lifting one message works. It closed on 15 Sep 2018. Blowhorn carries the idea forward with real people acting as themselves, at a human pace.

## Choose Thunderclap if

- Thunderclap is closed, so it can't be the fit today
- If you loved it for causes and campaigns, enterprise advocacy suites are the nearest living option at scale

- You want the Thunderclap feeling: many voices, one message

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

Thunderclap's lesson is that identical posts fired at the same minute look like a stampede, and platforms shut that down. Blowhorn's sound wave is different by design: people who chose to back you, acting as themselves, so the result is replies and discussion, not a wall of copies.
