# Blowhorn vs Blotato

> Blotato is an AI content engine with a hosted MCP that publishes to 9 networks through official APIs, and if you generate AI media at volume it earns its keep. Blowhorn isn't a bot. It's you: real-person amplification on developer networks, driven by your agent through a CLI.

## Choose Blotato if

- You generate AI media and video at volume
- You build in n8n or Make
- Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Facebook are core

[Blotato](https://www.blotato.com/)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- You want a post amplified by real people, not just generated and published
- Hacker News, Reddit, GitHub and Slack matter
- Your agent should drive scripted, rehearsable actions with --dry-run

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

Platforms are cracking down on bots while feeds fill with generated content nobody engages with. AI can make more content than any audience can read. The scarce thing is attention from people who care: likes don't move feeds. Blowhorn spends its effort there: your message, amplified by people who know you, with replies that turn into discussion.
