# Blowhorn vs ContentStudio

> ContentStudio is an AI-positioned cloud scheduler with a standout discovery engine and flat per-plan pricing. Blowhorn is for getting real people to amplify you on LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub, Bluesky and Slack.

## Choose ContentStudio if

- Your brand lives on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube
- You want a content discovery engine and flat per-plan pricing with unlimited users
- You want posting with every laptop closed

[ContentStudio](https://contentstudio.io/)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- Your audience is engineers on Hacker News, Reddit, GitHub, LinkedIn and X
- You want your team and supporters to amplify each post
- You want an agent-friendly CLI, not a visual grid

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

ContentStudio publishes from its cloud while your laptop stays closed. Blowhorn acts as you, from your own computer, and a single reply from someone who builds with you outweighs a pile of scheduled posts.
