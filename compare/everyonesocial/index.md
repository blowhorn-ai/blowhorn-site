# Blowhorn vs EveryoneSocial

> Blowhorn isn't a bot. It's you. EveryoneSocial is the leading employee advocacy platform for large organizations. Blowhorn brings the same 'people beat logos' idea to teams amplifying launches on the networks developers read.

## Choose EveryoneSocial if

- You're rolling advocacy out to hundreds or thousands of employees
- You need governance, compliance and earned media value reporting
- LinkedIn, X, Facebook and Instagram sharing at enterprise scale is the goal

[EveryoneSocial](https://everyonesocial.com/)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- You are a founder, a DevRel team, a practice, an agency or a growing company
- Your people talk on LinkedIn and X, and on Hacker News, Reddit, GitHub, Slack and Bluesky
- You want amplification working today, without a sales call or a seat-count quote

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

EveryoneSocial and Blowhorn agree on the big thing: people are the signal. The difference is whose computer does the talking. EveryoneSocial runs approved content from its cloud; Blowhorn acts from your own computers, under your own profiles, at a human pace - built for the feeds where developers decide what matters.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#questions}

**Is EveryoneSocial better than Blowhorn?** For enterprise advocacy with governance, yes - go use it. For a smaller team amplifying launches on the networks developers read, we think Blowhorn is the better fit.

**Can Blowhorn replace an advocacy program?** Not a thousand-seat program with compliance needs. For a team whose launch lives or dies in the first hour on LinkedIn, X, Reddit and Hacker News, Blowhorn is the lighter tool.
