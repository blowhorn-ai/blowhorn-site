# Blowhorn vs Hootsuite

> Blowhorn isn't a bot. It's you. Hootsuite is a full social operations suite for larger teams: inbox, listening, approvals, ads. Blowhorn is a lighter, sharper tool for technical voices who need real people amplifying, as themselves, on developer networks.

## Choose Hootsuite if

- You run a social team with approvals, a shared inbox, listening and paid boosts
- Compliance and governance features are a must
- You post mostly on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube

[Hootsuite](https://www.hootsuite.com/)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- You are a founder, DevRel lead or small team, not a social department
- Hacker News, Reddit, GitHub and Slack matter as much as LinkedIn and X
- You want your people to amplify a post as themselves, without enterprise seat math
- You run a local practice, an enterprise team, or several client brands from one shared queue

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

Hootsuite's boost is paid advertising, and ads are a fair, labeled trade. What Blowhorn adds is the thing ads can't buy: people who know you, replying and reposting because they actually care about what you shipped. When platforms crack down on anything that looks like a bot, a reply from a real person still reads as a person.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#questions}

**Is Hootsuite better than Blowhorn?** For a social team that needs one suite for inbox, listening, approvals and ads, yes. For a technical team amplifying launches on developer networks, we think Blowhorn fits better and costs far less.

**Does Blowhorn have a social inbox or listening?** No. It publishes, replies, amplifies and does careful LinkedIn outreach from your own computers.

**Can my team amplify without an Enterprise contract?** Yes. A sound wave amplifies from every profile in your Blowhorn account, with no enterprise tier needed. Hootsuite's advocacy product sits on Enterprise.
