# Blowhorn vs Typefully

> Typefully is the best writing experience for threads and posts on X, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky and Mastodon. Blowhorn takes over once it's written: real people amplifying it across many networks.

## Choose Typefully if

- Writing is the hard part, and you want a beautiful, distraction-free editor
- You mostly post as yourself on X and LinkedIn
- You want a polished cloud app for writing threads

[Typefully](https://typefully.com/)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- The post is written; now your team and supporters should amplify it
- You also need Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub and Slack
- You run several profiles and several computers from one queue

## Side by side

Typefully pricing as of Oct 2026: Free plan; paid plans for more posts, analytics and teams ([source](https://typefully.com/pricing)). Blowhorn: free plan available, see [pricing](https://blowhorn.ai/pricing/).

| | Typefully | Blowhorn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best at | Writing and scheduling | Amplifying and acting across networks |
| Where it acts | Typefully's cloud | Your computers: your own signed-in browser for LinkedIn, X, Reddit and Hacker News; the platform's API for GitHub, Bluesky and Slack |
| Networks | X, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon | LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub, Bluesky, Slack. Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube coming soon |
| Many people amplifying one post | Not across people | Sound wave: one scheduled message, amplified by every profile in your account, including profiles teammates and supporters add |
| AI agents | AI writing, hosted MCP and API | MCP server, AI skills and a full CLI with --json and --dry-run on writes. Your agent drives, you hold pause |
| Bought engagement | None | None. Blowhorn sells no followers, likes or reposts |
| Price | Free and paid plans | Free during early access |

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

Great writing earns attention. It earns a lot more when the people who know you reply and repost in the first hour. Typefully helps with the first half; Blowhorn is built for the second.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#questions}

**Is Typefully better than Blowhorn?** As an editor, yes, and we'd happily recommend it. For amplifying a post through many real profiles across many networks, we think Blowhorn is the better fit.

**Can I write in Typefully and amplify with Blowhorn?** Yes. Publish where you like, then point Blowhorn's amplify at the post.
