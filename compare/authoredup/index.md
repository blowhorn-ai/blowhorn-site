# Blowhorn vs AuthoredUp

> AuthoredUp is a lovely LinkedIn writing and analytics companion that stays in your browser. Blowhorn shares that your-own-browser belief: it isn't a bot, it's you, and it adds more networks, a fleet of computers and real-person amplification.

## Choose AuthoredUp if

- You want the best LinkedIn editor, previews and post analytics
- LinkedIn is your one channel
- You want zero automation anywhere near your LinkedIn account

[AuthoredUp](https://authoredup.com/)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- You also speak on X, Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub, Bluesky and Slack
- Your team and supporters should amplify your posts, from their own profiles
- You want one queue across every computer and profile, with one pause switch for all of them

## Side by side

AuthoredUp pricing as of Oct 2026: Individual $19.95 per month ($16.63 billed annually); Business from $14.95 per profile per month, 3+ profiles ([source](https://authoredup.com/pricing)). Blowhorn: free plan available, see [pricing](https://blowhorn.ai/pricing/).

| Bought engagement | None | None. Blowhorn sells no followers, likes or reposts |
| Price | Per profile | Free during early access |

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

AuthoredUp helps you write a post worth discussing. Blowhorn helps the discussion happen, through people who know you replying and reposting from their own profiles. AuthoredUp's own blog draws the line plainly: 'We do not distribute company content to employee accounts.'
