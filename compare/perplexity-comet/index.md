# Blowhorn vs Perplexity Comet

> Comet is a free AI browser with an assistant that can act on pages for you, and for research and everyday browsing it is genuinely good. Blowhorn isn't a bot. It's you: a dedicated social tool, scripted and scheduled, that works from your own browser across many profiles and computers.

## Choose Perplexity Comet if

- You want an AI-first browser for research and everyday tasks
- Occasional posting is enough
- You'd like to switch browsers

[Perplexity Comet](https://www.perplexity.ai/comet)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- You want queue, schedule, many profiles and a fleet of computers
- Actions should be scripted and rehearsable, not decided click by click
- Your team and supporters should amplify your posts

## Side by side

Perplexity Comet pricing as of Oct 2026: The browser is free; the assistant's agentic tasks use the free tier's limited credits, fuller access comes with Pro and Max plans, and the heavy automation is credit-metered through Perplexity Computer ([source](https://www.perplexity.ai/comet)). Blowhorn: free plan available, see [pricing](https://blowhorn.ai/pricing/).

| | Perplexity Comet | Blowhorn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | An AI browser | A dedicated social tool for your own browser |
| Scheduling | Recurring tasks via Perplexity Computer, cloud-run on the credit meter | A queue and schedule shared across every computer; any awake one picks up what is due |
| Many computers on one queue | No: Comet is one browser on your computer, heavier work hands off to Perplexity Computer in the cloud | Unlimited computers on one queue, coordinated so the same post isn't sent from two computers |
| Amplify one post | No | Sound wave: one scheduled message, amplified by every profile in your account, including profiles teammates and supporters add |
| How actions are decided | A model decides each step | Scripted actions, rehearsable with --dry-run |
| Bought engagement | None | None. Blowhorn sells no followers, likes or reposts |

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

Doing research by hand doesn't scale, which is what Comet is for. For a voice that matters, you want scripted, paced actions from your own signed-in browser and amplification by people who know you, because likes don't move feeds.
