# Blowhorn vs AdsPower

> AdsPower is an antidetect browser with RPA tools for ad and e-commerce teams. Blowhorn is a social tool for real people speaking as themselves.

## Choose AdsPower if

- You manage many ad or store accounts with separate fingerprints
- RPA workflows and a synchronizer help you
- Team permissions over shared profiles matter

[AdsPower](https://www.adspower.com/)

## Choose Blowhorn if

- You speak as real people, on accounts that are really theirs
- You want a social tool (queue, schedule, amplify, outreach), not a browser
- A real browser, signed in as you, is what you want

## Strong signal, not bought numbers

Blowhorn doesn't sell followers, likes or repost packages, and it never will. A like from someone paid to click is weak signal: it moves a number and nothing else. Strong signal is a reply, a question, a repost with a real take from someone who knows your work, a thread that turns into a discussion. Blowhorn exists to get your message in front of the people who would answer it, through the profiles of real people who chose to back you.

Separate identities are a legitimate need for ad ops, testing and e-commerce. Blowhorn isn't built for that. It's built for one honest idea: real people, as themselves, getting a conversation going about something they care about.
