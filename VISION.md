# Vision

Blowhorn Site exists so people can understand Blowhorn and reach its public information and downloads.
It serves people learning about, evaluating, or using Blowhorn through its website, published documentation, and releases.
It owns exactly one thing: Blowhorn's public web and download experience.

## A truthful public account

The site describes capabilities, availability, requirements, and limits only as specifically as public evidence allows.
When it discusses planned or pending work, it makes the status clear and does not present that work as available.
It gives visitors verified, approved pricing facts, including that Blowhorn is free during early access where that applies.
Other provisional plan facts appear only when useful, clearly labeled provisional, and approved by the product team; they are never presented as commitments.
It keeps plan prices, plan names, voice counts, and computer limits off pages until a plan ladder is adopted.

## A coherent visitor journey

Each page helps visitors understand the product or take a next step that fits their purpose.
The home page introduces the product and its integrations; the complete platform map stays on its dedicated page unless evidence shows a move improves the journey without harming clarity, performance, or accessibility.
Pages can offer several useful actions when they serve distinct needs, with a clear hierarchy and no competing primary actions.
When public content moves, established URLs and anchors continue to lead visitors to the closest relevant information.

## A recognizable Blowhorn identity

The public experience expresses Blowhorn's own identity rather than adopting a passing campaign look.
It can borrow a familiar pattern when that pattern improves comprehension or usability and serves Blowhorn's purpose.

## One reliable explanation

Repeated public facts have a defensible source and stay consistent wherever visitors encounter them.
Public instructions are corrected promptly when the information is verified and approved, while preserving one clear source of truth.
Technical documentation describes supported interfaces accurately, identifies their nature, and states their limits.
It does not imply HTTP or hosted behavior that Blowhorn does not offer, or turn internal and experimental material into a public promise.

## Trust through restraint

The site makes privacy, legal, support, and security information reachable when visitors need it.
It collects information for a clear purpose and explains where visitor data goes.
An external service earns a place only when its value justifies its privacy, performance, security, and maintenance costs.
The site prefers a less intrusive way to meet a need when one is practical and discloses data flows before sending visitor information to an external service.

## Scope

This project is not Blowhorn's desktop app, browser extension, or social publishing runtime.
It is not a promise that every planned capability is available.
It does not make the public site a substitute for product behavior or expose private implementation details.
It publishes public explanations, documentation, legal information, support paths, and download entry points for the product that exists.

A change aligns when it makes Blowhorn easier to understand or use, improves the accuracy of a public decision, or preserves a trusted path to information.
A change should be resisted when it blurs planned and available capabilities, presents unverified product or pricing claims, obscures visitor choices, weakens trust, or implies behavior the product does not provide.
