# Your team is the amplifier.

> Schedule one message and have every profile you add amplify it at the moment you pick: real teammates and supporters, each posting as themselves, in the first hour. No 100-seat minimum.

## Sound familiar?

- **Begging in Slack.** "Can everyone like this?" pings land after the moment has passed.
- **Suites priced for armies.** Employee advocacy suites are built for hundreds of seats, not a team with a launch day.
- **Nobody hands over their LinkedIn.** Teammates won't give a vendor their sign-in, and they shouldn't have to.

## What Blowhorn does for Marketing managers

- **Make a sound wave.** Queue the launch post and its reposts, quotes and reactions ahead of time. Every profile you add joins the same wave.
- **Their sessions stay theirs.** Each teammate's sessions stay in their own browser; Blowhorn acts as them there.
- **One pause for the whole wave.** Coordinated so the same post isn't sent from two computers. One command pauses everything.
- **One complete picture.** Run history and analytics from every computer in one place. Analytics cover LinkedIn, X and Bluesky.

## Campaign rows, queued and held for you

Your assistant can queue a row per campaign post. Blowhorn desktop 1.61.0 installs the blowhorn-mcp command in ~/.local/bin, so Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor or VS Code can drive Blowhorn on your own computer. With approval mode on, its queued work waits for you, and an agent can never approve its own content.

"Queue this week's campaign rows from campaign.csv for my company profile. Do a dry run, show me every row, wait for me to say "go", then confirm and list what is waiting for my approval."

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do teammates hand over their passwords?** No. Their sessions stay in their own browser, and Blowhorn acts as them there.

**Can I invite my team?** Today teammates and supporters add their own profiles to your account and join the wave. Multi-user team invites: Coming soon.

**What if a teammate's laptop is closed?** Their action waits in the queue for a computer set up for their profile, rather than going out wrong.

**Are there approvals or leaderboards?** Not today.
