# Rally the community. Everyone speaks for themselves.

> Announce to community Slack workspaces as yourself, then have maintainers and supporters amplify milestones from their own profiles in the first hour.

## Sound familiar?

- **Announcements that scroll away.** The milestone post is buried by lunch, in every channel at once.
- **A workspace at its app limit.** Free Slack plans cap installed apps, so a full workspace can't add another tool's bot.
- **Asking for likes.** Nobody enjoys sending the "please share this" message, or getting it.

## What Blowhorn does for Community managers

- **Slack as you, no bot to invite.** Sends as you through Slack's API, using your Slack session linked through the Blowhorn extension or a Slack token you add. Either one is kept in your Blowhorn account.
- **Supporters contribute their profile.** Supporters add their own profiles to your account and join the wave. Nobody acts for them.
- **Every channel in one queue.** LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Hacker News, Bluesky, GitHub and Slack from one queue.
- **Coordinated, with one pause.** Coordinated so the same post isn't sent from two computers. One command pauses everything.

## Rally the first hour, without the agent approving itself

Your assistant can plan and queue your teammates' first-hour engagement. Blowhorn desktop 1.61.0 installs the blowhorn-mcp command in ~/.local/bin, so Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor or VS Code can drive Blowhorn on your own computer. It can never approve its own content, and with approval mode on its queued work waits for you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does a supporter give me their password?** No. A supporter adds their own profile, signed in from their own browser.

**Does Blowhorn install a Slack app?** No. Blowhorn works on Slack through Slack's API, never the browser, and sends as you with your Slack session, linked through the Blowhorn extension, or a Slack token you add. Either one is kept in your Blowhorn account.

**Discord or Mastodon?** Not today. Blowhorn ships LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Hacker News, Bluesky, GitHub and Slack.
