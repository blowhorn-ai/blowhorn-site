# You're the company's voice. Keep its keys.

> Every launch, across your personal, company and project profiles, from one queue on computers you own. Blowhorn does the typing and clicking in your own browser, so nobody else holds the sign-ins that are your company.

## Sound familiar?

- **Launch week in tabs.** Personal, company and project profiles, each in its own tab, for every announcement.
- **A vendor holding your sign-ins.** The sessions that are your company end up on someone else's servers.
- **No time to babysit a schedule.** You have a company to run, not a posting calendar to watch.

## What Blowhorn does for Founders

- **Many profiles, one desk.** Every brand, founder and project profile you speak for, from one menu bar app, each in its own browser profile.
- **Your computers act.** Every action runs on one of your computers. Your account keeps the queue in sync; it never posts.
- **Any awake computer covers it.** Close your laptop. Another computer set up for that profile picks the schedule up.
- **Pause anything.** One command stops one action, one computer or all of them. Another starts it again.

## Launch day, with you in charge

Your assistant can line up your launch-day posts on your own profile while you stay in charge. Blowhorn desktop 1.61.0 installs the blowhorn-mcp command in ~/.local/bin, so Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor or VS Code can drive Blowhorn on your own computer. Every write is a dry run until you confirm, and pause stops everything.

"Prepare my launch-day posts from launch.md for my personal profile, spaced across the day. Do a dry run, show me the schedule, wait for me to say "go", then confirm and summarize what is queued."

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What lives in my account?** Your queue, schedule, profiles, run history and analytics, so every computer stays in sync. Cookies stay in the browser on each computer, and your account never posts.

**Will it post twice?** Your computers coordinate through one queue, so the same post isn't sent from two of them.

**Windows or Linux?** Not today. Blowhorn runs on macOS 13 or newer.
