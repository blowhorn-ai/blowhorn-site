- **Seven composers before breakfast.** Every day starts with seven sign-ins and seven composers, and the schedulers still skip Hacker News and GitHub.

## What Blowhorn does for Social media managers

- **One queue for every channel.** LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Hacker News, Bluesky, GitHub and Slack from one queue, posted as the company and as you.
- **Many profiles, one desk.** The company profile, your profile, the founder's: each its own browser profile, all from one menu bar.
- **A sound wave on launch day.** Schedule one message and have every profile you add amplify it at the moment you pick, in one coordinated blast.
- **Posted as you, never as a bot.** Blowhorn does the typing and clicking in your own browser, signed in as you. Nothing is branded sent via Blowhorn.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I post as the company or as myself?** Both. Each profile is its own browser profile on your computers, so the company post goes out as the company and your repost goes out as you.

**Does Blowhorn log in as me from its servers?** No. It does the typing and clicking in your own browser, signed in as you. No vendor server logs in as you.

**What about Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube?** Coming soon. Today the queue covers LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Hacker News, Bluesky, GitHub and Slack.

**Can my team amplify the launch?** Yes. Teammates and supporters add their own profiles to your account and join the wave. Multi-user team invites: Coming soon.
