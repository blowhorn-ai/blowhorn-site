# Ship the release. Announce it everywhere, as you.

> LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Hacker News, Bluesky, GitHub and Slack in one queue, posted from your own browser, signed in as you. Your agent drafts and dry-runs; you hold the switch.

## Sound familiar?

- **Seven tabs per post.** Every release means seven composers and seven sign-ins, and the schedulers still skip Hacker News and GitHub.
- **Tools that want your logins.** Outreach tools ask for your password, your 2FA secret or your session cookie, then log in as you from their servers.
- **A launch that lands quietly.** The post goes out, and the first hour is gone before the people who would amplify it notice.

## What Blowhorn does for DevRel

- **One desk for where developers talk.** Hacker News submit and comment, GitHub follow and react, Slack messages as you, plus LinkedIn, X, Reddit and Bluesky, from one queue.
- **Posted as you.** Blowhorn does the typing and clicking in your own browser. No vendor server logs in as you, and nothing is branded "sent via Blowhorn".
- **Your agent drives, you approve.** Every action is scripted and takes --dry-run first. No model decides what to click, and only approved posts go out.
- **A sound wave on release day.** Schedule the announcement and have every profile you add amplify it at the moment you pick.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Will it make me look like a bot?** Blowhorn types key by key at the pace you pick, caps itself (for example 2 Hacker News link submissions per profile per day) and always hands CAPTCHA and 2FA prompts to you. Nobody can promise a platform won't flag an account. Curious what sites can see about your browser? [Run the free check at Blowhorn Tells](https://tells.blowhorn.ai/)

**Does it vote on Hacker News?** Never. It submits stories and comments, nothing else.

**Does a computer have to be awake?** One set up for the profile does. Install Blowhorn on as many computers as you like; any awake one keeps the schedule.
