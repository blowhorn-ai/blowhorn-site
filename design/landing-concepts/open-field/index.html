How it works

You give the orders. Your Macs carry them out.

Blowhorn Cloud keeps your queue, profiles and run history in sync at cloud.blowhorn.ai. The work itself happens in the desktop app on machines you own.

  1. 01

    Write it once

    Draft the post in the Mac app, from the Python CLI, or let your agent draft it. Nothing goes out until you approve it.

    $ blowhorn post --check-queue --profile all
  2. 02

    Pick the moment

    Queue it for now or for launch o'clock. Add the reposts, quotes, comments and reactions your team will send after it. Preview any run first.

    $ blowhorn post --profile all --dry-run
  3. 03

    It goes out as you

    Your Mac does the typing and clicking in your own Chrome, at a human pace, signed in as you. Every result lands in Activity.

    $ blowhorn report

Trust

Yours to keep. Yours to control.

  • Human pace, always

    Blowhorn works at a pace you choose and never solves a CAPTCHA for you.

  • Never twice

    Your machines coordinate through one queue, so the same post isn't sent from two of them.

  • Hacker News, by the rules

    Submit and comment only. It never votes, and caps each profile at 2 link submissions a day.

  • Agents on a leash

    AI agents get the same queue and limits you do. Only approved posts go out.
