Everything Blowhorn does on X
Action What it does Limits and notes In the CLI
Post Publishes your message, with an image if you add one. A message longer than 274 characters goes out as a thread. X publishes 50 original posts a day for an account without a checkmark, and no setting in Blowhorn goes past it. blowhorn post --platform x --profile personal
ReplyRespond Replies in a conversation when your post is addressed to it. X publishes 200 replies a day for an account without a checkmark, and no setting in Blowhorn goes past it. X replies are not a distinct blowhorn command yet. Queue the tweet URL in Comment on.
Amplify Retweets or quotes a post from each of your profiles. None listed blowhorn post --platform x --amplify "https://x.com/user/status/123"
Follow Name a target account. With --limit 0, follow that account, or several comma-separated. With a positive limit, follow up to that many of the target's followers, never the target itself, skipping anyone you already follow. At most 400 follows per profile a day, and 15 in any 15 minutes, counted from that profile's follow log on this computer. blowhorn follow --platform x --profile personal --target mesheryio --limit 0
Analytics Records your follower and following counts on each run, so you can watch the trend. Read-only. blowhorn analytics history --platform x --json --since 12m

Rules of engagement

One choice sets your pace and daily caps on X.

  1. 01 12 Safety on posts a day on X Slow and careful.
  2. 02 25 Weapons tightDefault posts a day on X Normal pace, within the platform's own limits.
  3. 03 50 Weapons free posts a day on X Fast, and more noticeable to the platform.
  • Nothing goes past a limit X publishes.
  • A new profile or computer runs Safety on for its first 14 days.
