# Blowhorn Trust Center

> Many public voices, one coordinated queue, every action on your own computer. Every policy for the Blowhorn website, desktop app and Chrome extension, each with a plain-language summary. Where a summary differs from its policy, the policy decides.

## How Blowhorn handles your data

### Every action happens on your own computer

Blowhorn never acts for you from a server. Every post, comment and reaction is made by the Blowhorn app on one of your computers; the Chrome extension acts only when that app asks. [What stays on your computer](https://blowhorn.ai/legal/privacy/#on-your-machine)

### The extension reports to no server

The extension talks only to the Blowhorn app on your computer, over Chrome's native messaging. It acts only on supported sites, only in the Chrome profiles you install it in, and keeps nothing itself. [What the extension reads](https://blowhorn.ai/legal/privacy/#in-chrome-through-the-blowhorn-extension)

### No analytics in the app

The app carries no analytics or crash-reporting library. The one thing it sends about itself is failure reports, described on the privacy page. This website uses Google Analytics to count visits only if you accept its cookie notice, and those cookies are the only ones the site sets. You can change your choice under Cookie preferences. Its other outside request is the release lookup on GitHub. The newsletter form sends your email address to Blowhorn's own signup service only when you submit it. The privacy page describes all three. [What this website does](https://blowhorn.ai/legal/privacy/#this-website)

### One account for your queue, analytics and credentials

Your account keeps your queue, schedule, profiles, run history, analytics and platform credentials in sync, so any number of computers, yours or your teammates', run as one fleet. They share one queue, coordinated so the same post isn't sent from two of them. The account privacy policy governs that data. [What your account keeps](https://blowhorn.ai/legal/privacy/#in-your-blowhorn-account)

### You approve what goes out

Blowhorn publishes the rows you approve and the commands you run, from the accounts you configure. A dry run publishes nothing, and on Hacker News Blowhorn submits and comments only; it never votes. [Your accounts and your content](https://blowhorn.ai/legal/terms/#your-accounts)

### Releases you can check

The official downloads are signed disk images published with a SHA256SUMS.txt file to check them against, and a release is never replaced in place. The Blowhorn software is licensed under the AGPL-3.0. [Official downloads](https://blowhorn.ai/legal/terms/#downloads)

## Report a security issue

If you think you have found a security vulnerability in Blowhorn, send the details privately to [security@blowhorn.ai](mailto:security@blowhorn.ai) rather than opening a public issue. We acknowledge and analyze each report within 10 working days and keep the reporter updated while it is addressed. The [security policy](https://github.com/blowhorn-ai/blowhorn-site/blob/master/SECURITY.md) explains what to report and how fixes are disclosed.
