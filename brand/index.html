Speech bubbles

One bubble shape, four colour variants. The body and tail share one unbroken outline. Pick the variant by who is talking, not by what looks nice next to it.

  • Neutral: a quote or aside.

    Neutral (default)

    surface fill, near-black outline (cream in dark), ink-wordmark text. 9.3:1 light, 16.3:1 dark.

  • Paper: Major speaking.

    Paper

    cream fill, yellow-shade outline, charcoal text. 8.6:1.

  • Mint: what you say.

    Mint

    mint fill, teal-deep outline, charcoal text. 6.9:1.

  • Command: an order.

    Command

    teal-deep fill, no outline, white text. 5.6:1.

  • Paper is Major's own voice: his intros and asides.
  • Command is Major giving an order or a call to action. At most one per section.
  • Mint is what a person says to their agent, such as the Try saying prompts.
  • Neutral is for quotes and lists of lines, where colour would get noisy.
  • Paper, mint and command keep their colours in dark mode; neutral follows the theme.
  • Don't put links or buttons in a bubble, don't mix variants in one list, and don't draw a tail as a separate shape: use the bubble partial.

Cards

Five surfaces for cards, one for a floating panel. Pick the variant by what the content is, and never give two neighbours in a row or a column the same one. No card has a stripe on one edge.

  • Field order

    card-paper

    Major's voice, testimonials, the one featured item in a section. Cream paper, near-black outline, hard shadow. Keeps its colours in dark mode.

  • Dispatch

    card-dispatch

    Developer notes, API and JSON facts, commands. Near-black with a dashed teal edge and a mono prompt.

  • Stamp

    card-stamp

    Facts, stats, guarantees, proof points, compliance. No fill, a 2px ink edge, a boxed label.

  • Field

    card-field

    Feature and explanation grids, the common case. A flat tint with no edge.

  • Plain

    card-plain

    Secondary lists and link lists. No fill, one hairline on every side.

Glass

card-glass

The liquid glass surface, for one floating panel over content: a menu, the header, a hero. Never a card in a grid.

  • Every card link lifts 2px and brightens its edge on hover. There is no glow, and nothing moves under reduced motion.
  • Structure stays on the card (padding, radius, gaps). The variant only sets the look.
  • The full rules and contrast ratios are in DESIGN.md, "Cards".

Usage

The logo works hard. Let it work as drawn.

Do

  • Do: Full color on light grounds

  • Do: The on-dark file on dark grounds

  • Do: One color on teal and busy grounds

Don't

  • Don't: Recolor it

  • Don't: Stretch or squash it

  • Don't: Rotate it

  • Don't: Add shadows, glows or gradients

  • Don't: Set it on a busy ground

  • Don't: Put the full-color icon on teal

  • Blowhorn

    Don't: Retype the wordmark in a font

  • Don't: Rearrange the parts

And no retired marks or old names. If it isn't in the kit, it isn't the logo.

The name

Blowhorn. One word, capital B. Always just Blowhorn, in formal copy too. Never BlowHorn, never Blow Horn.

The tagline

One message out, many ears in. Set it in Bricolage Grotesque, sentence case, and let it stand on its own.
