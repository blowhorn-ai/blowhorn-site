# Meet Major Blowhorn

> Thirty years of basic training. One horn. Zero patience for messages nobody hears.

## Service record

- Rank: Major. Was Private, Corporal, Sergeant, then Major in one afternoon (see the Blowhard incident)
- Service: Thirty years running basic training. Now runs your queue
- Sidearm: Old Thunderlung, a dented brass trumpet. Never fired, only blown
- Breakfast: Push-ups. Fifty, sunny side up
- Medals: Order of the Early Worm, the Golden Comb, and a ribbon for Unnecessary Volume
- Pet peeve: Being called Major Blowhard
- Soft spot: The quiet recruit at the back with something worth saying

## The Blowhard incident

Young Private Blowhorn was told to whisper "lights out" to one barracks. He whispered into the camp megaphone and four counties went to bed. He was made Major on the spot, and the troops started calling him Major Blowhard. Not to his face.

## Old Thunderlung

His horn: a dented brass trumpet with a red tassel and one sticky valve, found in the henhouse rafters. "Is not instrument. Is colleague."

## Meet the troops

### The General

Outranks Major. Reminds him daily.

The old goat who promoted Major after the Blowhard incident, and he has been half deaf ever since. Now he holds a bugle to his ear and trusts only the numbers: real likes, replies and follows from LinkedIn, X and Bluesky, all in one report. He has never smiled. He came close once, at a reply count.

- [What the numbers mean](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/explanation/analytics/)
- [Get your analytics report](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/how-to/measure/analytics/#read-the-html-report)

### Robo-Recruit

Five tin cans. Zero improvising.

A squad of tin-can chickens who do exactly what the queue says, when it says, and not one thing more. They are not a bot farm with fake faces. They work inside your own browser, as you, and freeze for a human the second a CAPTCHA or a two-factor code shows up. The blank stares are a feature: imagination is not in their manual.

- [What Blowhorn will and won't do](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/explanation/youre-in-control/#blowhorn-limits-itself-to-what-a-human-could-do)

- [Preview a run before it posts](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/how-to/publish/post-content/#check-what-a-run-would-pick-up)

### Keyboard Warrior

Writes it once. Posts it everywhere.

A gold rooster with a keyboard for a shield. You write the post one time and he carries it to LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Bluesky, Slack, Hacker News and GitHub, no copy-paste marathon required. He will not improve your jokes. He just makes sure your words land where your people are.

- [How posting works](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/how-to/publish/post-content/)
- [Where you can post](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/reference/platforms/)

### Mouse Marine

Lands on every platform. Clicks where others can't.

A mallard riding a gold trackball on top of an amphibious mouse tank, cursor spear at the ready. When a site has no easy back door, he goes in the front, the way you would: through your own browser, clicking the real buttons. No ghost browsers on a stranger's server. When a CAPTCHA pops up, he salutes and hands it to you.

- [Why Blowhorn uses your own browser](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/explanation/your-own-chrome/)
- [What a website sees](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/reference/chrome/#what-a-site-sees-the-user-agent-by-driver)

### Sleeper Cell

Not sleepy. Just quiet. Very, very quiet.

Do not let the name fool you. This black-faced sheep has not slept since basic training. He guards your privacy from inside your own browser: each Blowhorn profile gets its own browser profile, and your logged-in sessions stay on your computer instead of in some vendor's cloud. His radar dish never stops sweeping, and the monocle is purely for intimidation.

- [One browser profile per Blowhorn profile](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/how-to/set-up/map-chrome-profiles/)

### Trench Trooper

Dug in against tab chaos since day one.

This mud-caked pig has seen things. Seven tabs to post one thing. The same announcement pasted until the clipboard begged for mercy. Now he holds the line with a tab bayonet, so one queue does the posting and your browser goes back to being a browser.

### Recruit Chick

Small. Loud. Armed with likes.

The smallest soldier with the biggest cannon. The moment your post goes live, she fires likes, reposts and comments from your other profiles, because early replies are what get a post seen. Every heart comes from a real account you own. No bought thumbs. Major checks.

- [Boost a post from your other profiles](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/how-to/publish/amplify-a-post/)
- [Which profiles can post](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/explanation/eligibility/)

### Platoon Private

Packs the posts. Brings the backup.

Backpack full of queued posts, bayonet fixed, never late for formation. He keeps one shared queue across every computer you own, and whichever one is awake picks up the next job. When your post goes out, your teammates' profiles show up to cheer, with no "hey, can everyone like this?" begging in Slack.

- [How your computers share one queue](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/explanation/scheduling/)
- [How teammates share one account](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/explanation/your-data/)

### Chief Drill Instructor

Short. Round. Furious about gaps in your calendar.

A stocky turkey with a weekly posting schedule he has laminated twice. Posting once in a blue moon isn't a strategy, it's a weather event. Line up the week and he blows the whistle when each post is due, so you keep showing up even on days you forget. He has never been thanked. He prefers it that way.

- [How scheduling works](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/explanation/scheduling/)
- [Schedule a post](https://blowhorn.ai/docs/how-to/schedule/schedule-a-job/)

### The G.I.

Pack mule. Carries your secrets. Tells no one.

A grumpy donkey hauling padlocked saddlebags full of your passwords. Other tools ask you to hand your logins to their cloud. He keeps them on your own computer and doesn't lend them out, not even to Major. Stubborn? Absolutely. That's the job description.

## Dispatches from the barracks

### 2026-10-12: Major hears the B word again

Somebody in Slack typed "Blowhard." Major is not angry. Major ran forty laps of the henhouse to show how not angry. Old Thunderlung also not angry. Old Thunderlung was very loud about it.

### 2026-10-08: Inspection of the reply section

Major counted the replies on Friday's launch. Thirty-one real humans, one real question, zero bought thumbs. Major cried a little. Allergies. Is dusty in barracks.

### 2026-10-01: Reveille, new recruits

New computers reported for duty this week. Major lined them up, read the rules, and told each one, "You post what queue says. Nothing more." They have not blinked since.
