# Comparisons

> Short films on how Blowhorn differs from a named tool, one competitor at a time.
- [Blowhorn vs Waalaxy](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-waalaxy/) - Waalaxy's cloud login asks for your password and your 2FA secret. Blowhorn keeps the session on your own computer, under your own profile. (0:36)
- [Blowhorn vs Typefully](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-typefully/) - Typefully writes and sends from the cloud. Blowhorn runs as you, on your own computer, under your own profile. (0:34)
- [Blowhorn vs Taplio](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-taplio/) - Taplio is a LinkedIn-only tool that hooks your live session. Blowhorn runs on your computer, under your own profile, across more than one network. (0:36)
- [Blowhorn vs Sprout Social](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-sprout-social/) - Sprout Social is a cloud suite, with advocacy sold as an add-on. Blowhorn is for getting real people to amplify you, on their own computers. (0:38)
- [Blowhorn vs SocialBee](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-socialbee/) - SocialBee runs evergreen queues in its cloud workspaces. Blowhorn runs as you, on your own computer, under your own profile. (0:34)
- [Blowhorn vs Social Browser](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-social-browser/) - Social Browser is a generic automation studio for any site, not a social operator. Blowhorn is the queue, the schedule, amplification and outreach in your real browser. (0:34)
- [Blowhorn vs Sociabble](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-sociabble/) - Sociabble, now Poppulo, is enterprise advocacy priced for large company suites. Blowhorn is a smaller, faster way for technical teams to amplify launches. (0:36)
- [Blowhorn vs Publer](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-publer/) - Publer schedules per account from its cloud. Blowhorn runs as you, on your own computer, under your own profile. (0:34)
- [Blowhorn vs Postiz](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-postiz/) - Postiz holds your OAuth token and publishes from its server. Blowhorn runs as you, on your own computer, under your own profile. (0:38)
- [Blowhorn vs Podawaa](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-podawaa/) - Reciprocal or bought engagement is not your crew. Blowhorn is real people, on their own computers, in their own words. (0:36)
- [Blowhorn vs PhantomBuster](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-phantombuster/) - PhantomBuster captures your session cookie and keeps running in its cloud after you close the browser. Blowhorn keeps the session on your computer, under your own profile. (0:36)
- [Blowhorn vs Multilogin](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-multilogin/) - Multilogin is fingerprints, proxies and infrastructure, not a social operator. Blowhorn is for real people posting as themselves. (0:36)
- [Blowhorn vs mokumokuren](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-mokumokuren/) - mokumokuren is local and careful, then the door stops. Blowhorn shares the your-computer model and adds the rest of the operator: more platforms, scheduling and a fleet. (0:36)
- [Blowhorn vs Metricool](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-metricool/) - Metricool's analytics live in the cloud, and its servers are what publish. Blowhorn publishes from your own computer, under your own profile. (0:34)
- [Blowhorn vs Lempod](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-lempod/) - A Lempod engagement pod is not a team. Blowhorn is a crew of real people, on their own computers, in their own words. (0:36)
- [Blowhorn vs Later](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-later/) - Later is a visual, Instagram-first planner; you still have to be the operator. Blowhorn is for technical launches that need real people in the replies. (0:34)
- [Blowhorn vs Hootsuite](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-hootsuite/) - Hootsuite holds tokens in its cloud, and advocacy sits behind Enterprise. Blowhorn is for real people posting as themselves, on their own computers. (0:38)
- [Blowhorn vs Haiilo](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-haiilo/) - Haiilo's advocacy starter starts at $15 a seat, with a 100-seat minimum that prices smaller teams out. Blowhorn is for teams that sit down at their own computers, as themselves. (0:38)
- [Blowhorn vs GoLogin](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-gologin/) - GoLogin is a fingerprinted cloud browser, not you in your own profile. Blowhorn acts as you, in your real browser. (0:34)
- [Blowhorn vs Expandi](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-expandi/) - Expandi takes your password and cookies and runs from its cloud. Blowhorn keeps actions on your own computer, under your own profile. (0:36)
- [Blowhorn vs EveryoneSocial](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-everyonesocial/) - EveryoneSocial is quote-based advocacy built for hundreds of seats. Blowhorn is for a team of people on their own computers, as themselves. (0:36)
- [Blowhorn vs DSMN8](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-dsmn8/) - DSMN8 is quote-based advocacy on an annual contract. Blowhorn is for small crews posting as themselves, on their own computers. (0:36)
- [Blowhorn vs Dripify](/pr-preview/pr-850/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-dripify/) - Dripify keeps running from its cloud after you close the lid. Blowhorn acts from your own computer, under your own profile. (0:36)
