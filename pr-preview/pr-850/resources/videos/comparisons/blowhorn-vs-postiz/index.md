# Blowhorn vs Postiz

> Postiz holds your OAuth token and publishes from its server. Blowhorn runs as you, on your own computer, under your own profile.

- Category: Comparisons
- Duration: 0:38
- Published: 2026-11-28

<p>A 38-second film from the Postiz comparison. Postiz is a cloud (or self-hosted) scheduler; the vendor server is what publishes with your OAuth token. Blowhorn coordinates in the cloud and never posts. Actions run on your own computer, under your own profile.</p>
