## Videos
- [Remote team, one army](/pr-preview/pr-797/resources/videos/features/remote-team/) - Every teammate runs Blowhorn on their own computer, signed in as themselves. You see the whole fleet and every result in one place.
- [Not a bot. It's you.](/pr-preview/pr-797/resources/videos/getting-started/not-a-bot/) - Platforms are done with bots. Blowhorn isn't one: it's you, on your own computers, under your own profiles, with your people beside you and you in command.
- [Beyond the API](/pr-preview/pr-797/resources/videos/features/beyond-the-api/) - Platform APIs keep some actions for approved partners. Blowhorn works the way you do, so it isn't limited to what those APIs expose.
- [Blowhorn vs Buffer](/pr-preview/pr-797/resources/videos/comparisons/blowhorn-vs-buffer/) - Buffer holds your token and publishes from its cloud. Blowhorn runs as you, on your own computer, under your own profile.
- [Battalion Thunder](/pr-preview/pr-797/resources/videos/getting-started/battalion-thunder/) - From one recruit to one thunder: install Blowhorn on as many computers as you like, they fall in as one unit, and one order goes out to every channel at once.
