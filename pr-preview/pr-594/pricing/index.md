# Pricing

> Start free, upgrade when you need more profiles or more platforms. Every plan posts from your own browser, as you, without handing anyone your password.

Yearly billing is ten months for twelve (2 months free).

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 forever | $0 forever | 1 profile; LinkedIn and X; Posts from your own browser; 1 computer |
| Solo | $19 a month | $190 a year | 3 profiles; Every supported platform; Scheduler that runs while you're away; Amplify a post from every profile; Basic LinkedIn invite tidy-up; Up to 2 computers |
| Pro | $39 a month | $390 a year | 10 profiles; Every supported platform; Scheduler that runs while you're away; Amplify a post from every profile; Full LinkedIn invite tidy-up (send, accept, withdraw); Up to 5 computers |
| Team | $35 a seat a month | $350 a seat a year | Up to 25 profiles for your organization; Every supported platform; Scheduler that runs while you're away; Amplify a post from every profile; Full LinkedIn invite tidy-up (send, accept, withdraw); Up to 3 computers per seat |

## Every plan includes

- **No passwords handed over.** Blowhorn posts from your own signed-in browser. We never ask for your password, cookies or two-factor codes.
- **No license keys.** Your plan lives in your account. Sign in on a computer and it just knows what you have.
- **Keeps going offline.** If your computer loses the internet, posting keeps working for up to 72 hours on your current plan.
- **Never locked out.** If a plan ends, nothing is deleted. Your queue, history and stats stay, and you drop to the Free limits.

## Our refund promise

On your first purchase you have 14 days to ask for a refund. If you haven't posted through Blowhorn yet, you get all of it back. If you have, we keep one month at the monthly price and refund the rest. After 14 days there are no cash refunds: your plan keeps working to the end of the year you paid for, then your account moves to Free.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#questions}

**Is the Free plan really free?** Yes. One profile on LinkedIn and X, for as long as you like. No card needed.

**What is a profile?** A profile is one person's set of social accounts, such as your own LinkedIn and X. Solo covers 3 people, Pro covers 10.

**How does yearly billing work?** You pay for ten months and get twelve. Solo is $190 a year, Pro is $390 a year and Team is $350 a seat a year.

**Can I get a refund?** Yes, within 14 days of your first purchase. If you haven't posted through Blowhorn yet, you get everything back. If you have, we keep one month at the monthly price and refund the rest. After 14 days there are no cash refunds.

**What happens when I cancel?** You can cancel anytime from Settings, which stops the next renewal. You keep everything you paid for until the end of your billing period, then your account moves to Free. Your posts and history stay.

**Can I change plans later?** Yes. Upgrades start right away and you only pay the difference for the rest of your term. A switch from yearly to monthly starts at your next renewal.

**Do I need a computer?** Yes. Blowhorn runs on your computer, Apple silicon or Intel, together with a small browser extension.
