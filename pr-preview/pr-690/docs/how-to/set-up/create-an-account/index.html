Create a Blowhorn account

Create your Blowhorn account, or sign in, from the desktop app's setup.

Your Blowhorn account lives in Blowhorn. The app's Sign in step has two buttons:

  • Sign in: opens your browser to sign in to an account you already have.
  • Create account: opens your browser so you can sign up.

Either way, the browser comes straight back to the app. There is no separate page asking you to allow the sign-in. The browser tab says Blowhorn received the answer: close it and return to the app, where the step has ticked off. The app never sees your password.

How Create account works today

  • Your browser opens Blowhorn's sign-up page. Sign in opens the sign-in page instead. Either one sends you back to the app when it finishes.
  • Google and GitHub work too: they always run in your browser.
  • Nothing to copy or paste: when sign-up finishes, the page hands you back to the app and the step ticks off.
  • Signing up with an email address adds a step: Blowhorn sends you a verification email. After you follow its link, the browser goes straight back to the app and the step ticks off by itself. You do not need to switch back to the app or press anything there. The wait for a sign-up is ten minutes, twice the sign-in wait. If the wait runs out anyway, your account is kept: verify your email, then sign in. You do not need to create the account again.

From the command line, blowhorn auth login --sign-up does the same thing.
