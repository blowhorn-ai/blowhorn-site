# LinkedIn hygiene, at human pace.

> Invite people to your page, connect mentees, accept invitations and withdraw stale invites, in your own browser, signed in as you. No password handed to a server, and no full outbound sequencer: flagged invitations are never auto-accepted.

## Sound familiar?

- **Invites pile up.** Stale invitations sit for months, and the safe ones wait in the same pile as the risky ones.
- **Your login on their server.** Growth tools ask for your password, your 2FA secret or your session cookie, then sign in as you from their cloud.
- **A sequencer you don't need.** You want light LinkedIn hygiene, not a high-volume outbound funnel.

## How Blowhorn runs it

- **Your browser, as you.** Blowhorn does the typing and clicking in your own browser profile. No vendor server logs in as you.
- **Human-paced by design.** Types key by key, pauses between actions and caps itself, with 20 LinkedIn invitations per run. CAPTCHA and 2FA always come to you.
- **Flagged invitations skipped.** Accept the safe ones and withdraw the stale ones. Flagged invitations are never auto-accepted.
- **Light outreach, nothing more.** Invite to your page, connect mentees, accept and withdraw. Not a full outbound sequencer, and we don't pretend to be one.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this for high-volume outbound or SDR teams?** No. This page is for founders, DevRel and consultants doing light LinkedIn hygiene. High-volume outbound and SDR teams are out of fit: Blowhorn caps itself, moves at human pace, and is not a full outbound sequencer.

**Will flagged invitations be accepted?** Never auto-accepted. Flagged invitations are skipped; you accept the safe ones and withdraw the stale ones.

**Who signs in to LinkedIn?** You do, in your own browser. Blowhorn types and clicks there, and no password, 2FA secret or session cookie is handed to a vendor's server.

**Is it an outbound sequencer?** No, and it doesn't pretend to be one. It covers careful outreach: invite to your page, connect, accept and withdraw.
