Support

How can we help?

Questions, ideas and feedback are welcome. Send a quick note anonymously, or leave your email for a response.

Send us a message

Report a problem, ask a product question or just say hello. Leave an email only if you want a response.

Anonymous means no name or email is saved. The service still receives your IP address. Leave identifying details out of your message.

Required only if you choose a response.

Add diagnostics (optional)

Have a case or report reference from the team or the CLI? Add it here. To send a diagnostics package, use the Blowhorn CLI instead: it builds a package you review, then submits it with your report. This form does not upload files.

Prefer email?

support@blowhorn.ai

A security problem goes to security@blowhorn.ai, never the form or a public issue.

The form sends your message and response preference to our support queue. Name and email are optional. The privacy page says what is stored and where it goes.

Let your AI help with the details

For a problem report, copy this prompt into the AI you use with Blowhorn. Review the summary before adding it to your message above.

Blowhorn can preview small, redacted failure reports. This form does not yet accept diagnostic archives or upload files. A local report ID is not proof that a report reached the team.
