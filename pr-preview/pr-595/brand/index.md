## Clear space and minimum size

- Clear space: half the icon's height on every side.
- Minimum sizes: horizontal lockup 96 px wide, stacked lockup 64 px, icon 24 px. Below that, use the favicons.

## Color

### Core

- Teal: #00B39F, rgb(0, 179, 159). The primary fill: the horn, buttons, big moments. Not for small text on light grounds.
- Charcoal: #3C494F, rgb(60, 73, 79). The wordmark on light grounds and every outline.
- Near Black: #1E2117, rgb(30, 33, 23). The deepest ink and the dark ground.
- White: #FFFFFF, rgb(255, 255, 255). The wordmark on dark grounds; text on charcoal and near black.

### Accent

- Teal Bright: #00D3A9, rgb(0, 211, 169). Highlights. Links and focus on dark grounds.
- Teal Deep: #00756A, rgb(0, 117, 106). Text-safe teal on light grounds: links and focus.
- Yellow: #EBC017, rgb(235, 192, 23). The accent. Keep it under a tenth of any layout. Never text on light.
- Coral: #EE5A44, rgb(238, 90, 68). The rooster's comb and wattle, and a brand accent.
- Cream: #FFF6E0, rgb(255, 246, 224). Warm grounds for badges and Major Blowhorn.

### Illustration

- Teal Shade: #008F80, rgb(0, 143, 128). Shading on teal; the hover on teal fills.
- Mint: #A6EBDF, rgb(166, 235, 223). Major's chest.

- Yellow Shade: #C99F00, rgb(201, 159, 0). Shading on yellow.
- Coral Shade: #C2412F, rgb(194, 65, 47). Shading on coral.
- Khaki: #C9995A, rgb(201, 153, 90). The campaign hat.

## Old Thunderlung

Major Blowhorn's family bugle. Six generations of Blowhorns have carried it, and it has the dent to prove it.

It only sounds for real messages to real ears, so bots and bought crowds can't hear it. Nobody else plays it. As Major puts it: "Is not instrument. Is colleague."

## Usage

Do: full color on light grounds; the on-dark file on dark grounds; one color on teal and busy grounds.

Don't: recolor, stretch, squash or rotate the logo; add shadows, glows or gradients; set it on a busy ground; put the full-color icon on teal; retype the wordmark in a font; rearrange the parts; use retired marks or old names.

The name is Blowhorn: one word, capital B. Always just Blowhorn, in formal copy too.
