  • Their own IP address

    Each post leaves from the computer that runs it, over that teammate's own internet connection. Your account never posts.

  • No proxy bill

    No proxy or dedicated IP to buy for each account. The internet your team already has is the one Blowhorn uses.

  • Nothing extra to run

    No proxy pool to rent, assign, rotate or keep healthy, and no proxy setting to get wrong.

  • Lower risk

    No proxy or data center address for a site to flag. Each teammate's own connection is the one the platform already knows.

A hard cap above every stop

Blowhorn follows its Field Manual and each platform's best practices. Every cap sits under Blowhorn's own ceiling for that platform, and above everything stands a hard cap from the limits the platform itself publishes. No stop, not even Weapons free, pushes past them and puts your accounts at risk. Your own caps can only lower them, never raise them.
