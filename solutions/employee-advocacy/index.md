# Advocacy for the rest of us.

> Real teammates, posting as themselves, from one coordinated queue. No 100-seat minimum.

## Sound familiar?

- **The hundred-seat fortress.** Advocacy suites sell to big companies: quote pricing and seat minimums, like Haiilo's 100 seats.
- **A library nobody opens.** Teammates get an email with a post to share. By the time they do, the first hour is gone.
- **Built around LinkedIn and X.** Suites center LinkedIn and X. Your audience is also on Hacker News, Reddit, GitHub and Slack.

## What Blowhorn does instead

- **A sound wave from your team.** Schedule one post and every profile you add amplifies it at the moment you pick, each posting as itself.
- **Teammates bring their own profiles.** Teammates and supporters add their own profiles to your account today. Team invites: Coming soon.
- **Sent once, from one computer.** Coordinated so the same post isn't sent from two computers, however many your team runs.
- **One pause for the whole wave.** Pause one action, one computer or every computer at once. Resume, and due times carry forward.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Blowhorn post for teammates without them?** No. A teammate adds their own profile to your account, and Blowhorn posts as that person, never as a bot.

**Can I invite my team?** Team invites are Coming soon. Today teammates and supporters add their own profiles to your account.

**Will the same post go out twice?** Your computers coordinate through one queue, so the same post isn't sent from two of them.

**Do teammates need a proxy?** No. Each teammate posts from their own computer's internet connection, with their own IP address. There is no proxy to buy or set up.
