# Post where developers actually talk.

> Hacker News, GitHub, Reddit and Slack, plus LinkedIn, X and Bluesky, from one queue. Posted as you, at a human pace.

## Sound familiar?

- **Schedulers skip where developers talk.** None of the schedulers we checked support Hacker News. GitHub and Slack stay a pile of tabs.
- **Developers spot a bot.** Votes, blast posts and "sent via" badges cost the trust you came to earn.
- **A Slack app nobody can install.** Assistants that act as themselves need an app in the workspace. A free workspace at its app limit has no room.

## What Blowhorn does instead

- **Every developer network, one queue.** Hacker News submit and comment, GitHub follow and react, Slack messages, plus LinkedIn, X, Reddit and Bluesky.
- **Never votes on Hacker News.** It submits stories and comments, nothing else, with at most 2 link submissions per profile per day.
- **No Blowhorn app in your Slack.** Blowhorn works through your own Slack sign-in and posts as you. No app to install, no bot user to invite.
- **Human-paced by design.** Types key by key in your own browser, caps itself, and hands every CAPTCHA and 2FA prompt to you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does it vote on Hacker News?** Never. It submits stories and comments, nothing else. Link submissions are capped at 2 per profile per day.

**Does Blowhorn install a Slack app?** No. Blowhorn works on Slack through Slack's API and sends as you, with your Slack session linked through the Blowhorn extension or a Slack token you add.

**How does it post to each network?** LinkedIn, X, Reddit and Hacker News: Blowhorn types in your own browser, signed in as you. Slack, Bluesky and GitHub go through their APIs.
