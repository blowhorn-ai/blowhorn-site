# A scheduler that posts as you.

> Queue it once, like any scheduler. The difference is who presses Post: their servers, or your computers.

## Sound familiar?

- **Their app, your name.** Cloud schedulers post through the vendor's app. You hand over access and they press Post.
- **Half the channels by hand.** Hacker News, GitHub and Slack still happen in tabs, outside the calendar.
- **Posted once, then quiet.** Most schedulers publish one post. The profiles that would amplify it are left to remember.

## What Blowhorn does instead

- **Your computers press Post.** Every action runs on one of your computers, in your browser or through the platform's API. Your account keeps them in sync and never posts.
- **Every developer channel, one queue.** Hacker News, GitHub and Slack join LinkedIn, X, Reddit and Bluesky in one queue.
- **Amplify from your profiles.** Schedule a post, then have every profile you run amplify it at the moment you pick.
- **Any awake computer covers it.** Close your laptop. Another computer set up for that profile picks it up, as long as one of them is awake.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does a computer have to be awake?** One set up for the profile does. As long as one of your computers is awake, it covers the schedule. Each checks the queue about every 10 minutes.

**Where does the schedule live?** In your Blowhorn account, so every computer you sign in on works from the same queue. Your account coordinates; it never posts.
