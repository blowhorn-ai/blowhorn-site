# LinkedIn automation without handing over your login.

> Blowhorn works LinkedIn in your own browser, signed in as you, at a human pace. No server logs in as you.

## Sound familiar?

- **Your login on their server.** Growth tools ask for your password, your 2FA secret or your session cookie, then sign in as you from their cloud.
- **"Bypass the limits".** Some outreach tools sell going past LinkedIn's limits. Your account carries the risk.
- **A sequencer you don't need.** You want careful LinkedIn hygiene, not a high-volume outbound funnel.

## What Blowhorn does instead

- **Your browser, as you.** Blowhorn does the typing and clicking in your own browser profile. No vendor server logs in as you.
- **Human-paced by design.** Types key by key, pauses between actions and caps itself. CAPTCHA and 2FA always come to you.
- **Careful outreach.** Invite to your page, connect, accept invitations and withdraw stale ones. Flagged invitations are never auto-accepted.
- **Posts and amplify too.** Publish, reply and amplify on LinkedIn from the same queue as X, Reddit, Hacker News and the rest.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is it ban-proof?** No tool is. Blowhorn moves like a careful person, caps itself and stops for any challenge. Nobody can promise LinkedIn won't flag an account.

**Is it an outbound sequencer?** No, and it doesn't pretend to be. It covers careful outreach: invite to your page, connect, accept and withdraw.

**Who signs in to LinkedIn?** You do, in your own browser. Blowhorn types and clicks there, and LinkedIn's session cookies stay in the browser on each computer.
